Nursery, primary and secondary under one roof

Choose a school to see what your child will learn and how we support them.

Nursery and Primary, ages 3 to 11

Children learn to read, write and count with confidence in small classes. Learning is hands-on, and every day begins with prayer and a short Bible story.

Each pupil has a class teacher who knows their strengths and what they find hard, and tells parents about both.

  • Phonics-led reading and handwriting
  • Mathematics, English, Science and Computing
  • Music, art, sport and French club
  • Weekly library and storytelling time
  • Before- and after-school care

Secondary School, ages 11 to 18

Students build strong subject knowledge and prepare for national examinations. Each student has a mentor who meets them weekly and helps them plan their next step after school.

Leadership, service and character are part of the timetable, not extras.

  • Core subjects plus Languages, ICT and Business
  • Separate Biology, Chemistry and Physics
  • Career guidance and university applications
  • Debate, robotics, choir and sports teams
  • Community service every term
